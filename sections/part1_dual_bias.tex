\subsection{Dual-drain-bias transfer characteristics (22 October 2026)}
The baseline SProcess run (node \texttt{n5}) supplied the same SOI nMOSFET
geometry to the two completed SDevice transfer simulations:
$V_{DS}=0.1$ V (node \texttt{n3}) and $V_{DS}=1.2$ V (node \texttt{n9}).
Both \texttt{.plt} datasets and solver logs were produced. The exported
\texttt{Ig\_Vg\_n3\_n9.csv} was aligned by gate voltage, excluding repeated
initial drain-ramp points at $V_{GS}=-0.5$ V. The resulting 34-point
paired sweep is archived as
\texttt{data/idvg\_vds\_0p1\_1p2\_comparison.csv}.

Threshold voltage is defined here \emph{operationally} by the constant
current criterion $I_D=10^{-7}$ in the exported current units.
$V_{\mathrm{TH,CC}}$ is interpolated linearly in $\log_{10}(I_D)$ between
adjacent sweep points. This chosen reference current has not been normalized
to channel width. Subthreshold swing is calculated from a common least-squares
fit to $\log_{10}(I_D)$ for $-0.20\leq V_{GS}\leq0.05$ V (six samples).
The earlier eight-point fit for the single 0.1-V curve gave
$63.61$ mV/dec; the common-window fit below is preferable for comparison.

The matched-definition drain-induced barrier lowering is
\[
\mathrm{DIBL}_{\mathrm{CC}}=
\frac{V_{\mathrm{TH,CC}}(0.1)-V_{\mathrm{TH,CC}}(1.2)}
{1.2-0.1}
=\frac{0.1774277-0.1593354}{1.1}
=\boxed{16.45\ \mathrm{mV/V}}.
\]
At $V_{GS}=1.2$ V, raising $V_{DS}$ from 0.1 to 1.2 V increases the
exported current by a factor of 3.24, while the corresponding
$V_{GS}=0$ V current increases by 1.98. This is a baseline TCAD
comparison, not yet a width-normalized measured device specification.
The supplied separate $I_D$--$V_D$ family remains pending.
