# Available Sentaurus figures

The following original Sentaurus visualization PNGs are **already committed** and used in the report:

- `02_extension.png` — gate/arsenic extension implant
- `03_spacer.png` — oxide spacer
- `04_annealed.png` — deep source/drain implant and anneal
- `05_final.png` — final contact/mesh
- `part2_potential_comparison.png` — two matched 2D potential maps at VGS=0 V and VDS=1.2 V

The initial stack and gate-oxide stages are covered by **three genuine cropped SVisual screenshots**: `00_initial_soi_svisual.png`, `01_gateoxide_full_svisual.png`, and `01_gateoxide_zoom_svisual.png`. The original images were cropped and downsampled for the repository without changing plot values. They are embedded by `sections/part1_initial_svisual_screenshot.tex` and `sections/part1_gateoxide_svisual_screenshots.tex`. Two separate data-reconstructed, to-scale TikZ figures appear in `sections/part1_initial_soi_profile.tex` and `sections/part1_gateoxide_profile.tex` and should not be mistaken for 2D cross sections. The two baseline Id–Vg figures are vector plots generated from the archived CSV data rather than screenshots.

Numerical measurements are derived from exported Sentaurus data, not by measuring plot screenshots.
